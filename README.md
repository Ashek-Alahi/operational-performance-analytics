> This is a simulated ERP-style hospitality analytics project. It models hotel operations, billing, accounts receivable, department expenses, labor scheduling, and daily sales to produce management KPIs and dashboard-ready outputs.

## Business Problem

Hotel managers often review revenue, costs, labor, and receivables in separate reports. This makes it difficult to answer practical management questions quickly:

- Which months drive the highest occupancy and profit?
- Which revenue categories create the most value?
- Which departments create the largest cost pressure?
- Are labor hours aligned with seasonal demand?
- Which customers and aging buckets require collection action first?

This project consolidates those topics into reproducible CSV, SQL, Markdown, and static HTML outputs for management reporting.

## Data Disclaimer

The dataset is fully simulated with Python. It is not from a real hotel, real company, SAP system, PMS, or live ERP connection. It is designed to resemble ERP/PMS/finance/operations exports for portfolio demonstration and should not be presented as real operational data. See `docs/data_disclaimer.md`.

## KPI Definitions

| KPI | Formula / Logic | Business Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Occupancy Rate % | Occupied room nights ÷ available room nights | Measures utilization and seasonal demand |
| ADR | Room revenue ÷ occupied room nights | Tracks pricing performance |
| Revenue by Category | Sum of daily sales by category | Shows which services drive revenue |
| Cost by Department | Sum of monthly department expenses | Identifies cost-heavy departments |
| AR Aging | Outstanding balances grouped into Paid, 0-30, 31-60, and 60+ days | Prioritizes collection action |
| Monthly Profit Margin % | Operating profit ÷ revenue | Measures monthly financial performance |
| Outstanding Receivables | Sum of unpaid invoice balances | Quantifies cash collection exposure |
| Labor Hours | Scheduled hours by department and date | Supports staffing and productivity analysis |
| Revenue per Labor Hour | Revenue ÷ labor hours | Measures staffing productivity |

## ERP and SAP Relevance

This project is ERP-relevant because it uses common business objects: bookings/orders, invoices, AR balances, cost centers/departments, labor activity, and sales categories. It is useful for discussing SAP-style business process thinking across finance, controlling, operations, and reporting.

It is not an SAP S/4HANA implementation. It does not connect to SAP, configure SAP modules, or use real SAP tables. The value is in showing how ERP-style operational data can be modeled, validated, queried, and translated into management KPIs.

## Power BI Positioning

This repository includes Power BI-ready DAX measures and a dashboard design plan. It does not include a `.pbix` file. The included `dashboard/executive_dashboard.html` is a static HTML preview generated by `analysis/generate_results.py`, not a Power BI report.

## Limitations

- Not a production forecasting system.
